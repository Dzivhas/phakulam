Statutory & Regulatory Governance

Compliance disclosures in terms of LPC, POPIA, FICA, and PAIA enactments.

Legal Practice Council (LPC) Disclosures

Phakula M Attorneys is a sole legal practice operating under the regulatory oversight of the Legal Practice Council (Gauteng Provincial Office) pursuant to the Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014.

Fidelity Fund Certificate

In compliance with Section 84 of the Legal Practice Act, practicing attorneys of the firm hold valid Fidelity Fund Certificates, ensuring the public protection of money deposited into our trust banking account.

Contingency Fee Agreements

Any speculative or contingency fee arrangements adhere strictly to the Contingency Fees Act 66 of 1997. Formal written agreements are executed setting out fee caps prior to litigation commencing.

Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)

Phakula M Attorneys complies with Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) in safeguarding personal information gathered via consultations, web forms, and legal correspondence.

Data Processing Principles

  • Personal data is processed strictly for legal advice, representation, and client billing.
  • We do not transfer or sell client data to third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Clients retain the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information subject to legal record retention statutes.

Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA)

As an accountable institution under Schedule 1 of the Financial Intelligence Centre Act 38 of 2001, we are required to conduct Customer Due Diligence (CDD) prior to establishing a business relationship.

Verification Documents Required

All clients must furnish certified South African Identity documents, valid proof of physical address, and SARS tax numbers where applicable prior to mandate execution.

Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA) & Disclaimers

Our PAIA Manual is available for inspection upon request at our physical premises in accordance with Section 51 of Act 2 of 2000.
