About Phakula M Attorneys

Our background, legal qualifications, and statutory commitment to legal excellence.

Firm Overview

A Modern South African Law Practice Driven by Principles

Phakula M Attorneys is a dedicated legal firm headquartered in Roodepoort, South Africa. We deliver specialized legal representation across civil litigation, family law, labour disputes, and criminal defence.

Founded on the principles of access to justice, legal accountability, and uncompromising ethical standards, our practice serves clients across the Magistrate’s and High Courts of Gauteng.

Ethical & Regulatory Standard

We are fully registered with the Legal Practice Council (LPC) and maintain an active Legal Practitioners' Fidelity Fund Certificate, guaranteeing that all client trust money is safeguarded in terms of Section 86 of the Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014.

Legal Qualifications & Framework

  • Jurisdiction: Republic of South Africa
  • Regulatory Body: Legal Practice Council (Gauteng)
  • Admission: Admitted Attorney of the High Court of South Africa
  • Academic Background: Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.)
  • Statutory Compliance: Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014, POPIA Act 4 of 2013, FICA Act 38 of 2001
