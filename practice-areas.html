Legal Services & Practice Areas

Expert legal guidance across South African statutory, civil, and criminal law.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

1. Family & Matrimonial Law

Divorce Act & Children's Act 38 of 2005

Family law requires both legal expertise and compassionate advocacy. We guide clients through the legal complexities of domestic relations in South African courts.

  • Contested & Uncontested Divorces in the Regional Court and High Court
  • Drafting of Parenting Plans and Parental Rights & Responsibilities agreements
  • Child and Spousal Maintenance claims and enforcement
  • Domestic Violence Protection Orders and Urgent Interdicts
📜

2. Civil & Commercial Litigation

High Court & Magistrate's Court

Providing robust court representation for contractual, financial, and property disputes.

  • Contractual breach claims and damages litigation
  • Commercial debt recovery and warrant execution
  • Eviction applications under the Prevention of Illegal Eviction (PIE) Act
  • Interdicts and urgent court applications
🏛️

3. Criminal Law Defence & Bail Applications

Criminal Procedure Act 51 of 1977

Protecting constitutional rights at every phase of criminal investigation and prosecution.

  • 24/7 Police Station attendance and Urgent Bail Applications
  • Formal Bail Proceedings in Magistrate's Courts
  • Trial defence for statutory and common law offences
  • Expungement of criminal records where permissible by law
💼

4. Labour & CCMA Matters

Labour Relations Act & BCEA

Representing employees and employers in workplace disputes and compliance audits.

  • Unfair Dismissal and Unfair Labour Practice referrals to the CCMA / Bargaining Councils
  • Representation at Disciplinary Hearings and Arbitrations
  • Drafting employment policies, contracts, and restraint of trade agreements
📑

5. Wills & Deceased Estates Administration

Administration of Estates Act 66 of 1965

Ensuring your estate is structured legally and administered seamlessly without delay.

  • Professional drafting of legally compliant Wills and Codicils
  • Reporting and Winding up of Deceased Estates with the Master of the High Court
  • Registration of Antenuptial Contracts (ANC) with Notarial Vetting
